Who We Are

Bible-believing.
Christ-centered.
Deep roots.

"We loved you so much that we were delighted to share with you not only the gospel of God but our lives as well." — 1 Thessalonians 2:8

We are an Independent Baptist church. Our focus is to magnify the creator God and His son Jesus Christ — proclaiming to the world that Christ died for the sins of mankind as an atonement to reconcile us to the Father. Come as you are.

Expository Preaching

The Bible,
straight and unvarnished.

Every sermon is rooted directly in Scripture — verse by verse, book by book. We believe the Word of God is living, active, and sufficient. You'll leave each Sunday knowing exactly what the text says and why it matters for your life today.

"All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness."2 Timothy 3:16
🎵
Full Choir · Old Hymns

Singing the hymns
the way they were meant.

Our full choir leads worship every Sunday through the timeless hymns of the faith — rich in doctrine, deep in feeling, and lifted together by the whole congregation. There's nothing like the sound of a church family singing as one.
