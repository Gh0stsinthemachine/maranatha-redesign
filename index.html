Who We Are

Casual clothes.
Real conversations.
Deep roots.

"We loved you so much that we were delighted to share with you not only the gospel of God but our lives as well." — 1 Thessalonians 2:8

We're a non-denominational church that believes Sunday morning is just the beginning. Faith grows in community — in kitchens, on phones, in small groups, and in the everyday rhythms of life. Come as you are.

Age-Specific Programs

Faith at every
stage of life.

From infant care to young adulthood, Maranatha walks alongside you and your family wherever you are.

Youngest

Maranatha Kids · Nursery

Safe, loving childcare for your littlest ones so you can fully engage in the Sunday service. Staffed by trained, caring volunteers.

Ages: 6 months – 3 years
When: During Sunday service
Kids

Maranatha Kids · Classes

Age-appropriate biblical teaching for school-age kids during the Sunday sermon. Fun, engaging, and grounded in Scripture.

Ages: 4 years – 6th grade
When: During Sunday service
Youth

Youth Group

A dedicated Sunday evening gathering for middle and high schoolers in The Garage — a space that's theirs to explore faith, ask questions, and build community.

Grades: 7th – 12th
When: Sunday · 6:00 PM

Young Adults Community
